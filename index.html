Meet Nikki, Your Artist

Hi, I'm Nikki, the founder behind Nikki Studio. Operating from a private, beautifully clean home studio in West Swindon, SN5, I recognized that commercial high street nail bars often compromise on quality and structural preservation just to churn through client volumes as quickly as possible.

I built this dedicated studio to reverse that approach. Here, nail design is treated as a form of personalized fine art and hygienic micro-therapy. We work strictly on a custom 1-on-1 basis. That means tools are cleaned and disinfected following professional BARBICIDE® sanitation and disinfection procedures, you get fully personalized style mappings, and we never use rush tactics that damage or strip your natural nail plate.

We rely entirely on elite non-toxic brands, allowing us to generate beautiful finishes that stay gorgeous for up to 4-6 solid weeks. Whether you are seeking a customized set of sculpted extensions or looking to relax with a deeply rejuvenating pedicure, my doors are open for your private sanctuary experience in Swindon.
